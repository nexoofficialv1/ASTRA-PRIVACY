Privacy Policy — ASTRA GEO CAM

Effective date: 23 August 2026

Last updated: 23 August 2026

ASTRA GEO CAM is a camera application designed to capture photographs and videos together with user-relevant geographic and capture information where those features are enabled.

Camera Access

ASTRA GEO CAM requires camera access to capture photographs and videos. Camera access is used only for user-initiated capture functionality.

Location Information

When location-based capture features are used, ASTRA GEO CAM may access device location information such as latitude, longitude and related location data. This information may be displayed on or associated with captured media to provide geographic context.

Location access is used only when required for location-enabled application features. Users can manage location permissions through Android system settings.

Photos, Videos and Device Storage

The application may create, save, read or share photographs and videos on the user's device as required for capture, preview, saving and sharing functionality. Media created by the application may remain on the device until the user deletes it.

Microphone Access

When video recording with audio is used, ASTRA GEO CAM may access the device microphone to record sound together with the video.

Device Permissions

Depending on the Android version and features used, the application may request permissions related to camera, microphone, location, media or storage. Permissions are requested only for features that require them.

Local Processing

Core capture and media-processing functions are designed to operate primarily on the user's device. Captured media and associated information are generally stored locally unless the user explicitly uses a sharing or external-service feature.

Sharing

If a user chooses to share a photograph, video or related information, the selected content may be passed to another application or service chosen by the user. The receiving application or service is governed by its own privacy practices.

Personal Information

ASTRA GEO CAM is not designed to require account registration for its core camera functionality. The application does not intentionally collect unnecessary personal information solely for advertising or profiling purposes.

Advertising and Tracking

The core application experience is not designed to require behavioural advertising or unnecessary cross-app tracking.

Data Security

Reasonable safeguards are used in the design of the application. Users remain responsible for protecting access to their devices and any photographs, videos or location-related information stored on them.

Children's Privacy

ASTRA GEO CAM is a general-purpose camera utility and is not specifically designed to solicit personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

Some device-level sharing, mapping, storage or operating-system functionality may rely on third-party applications or platform services. Their respective privacy policies may apply when users choose to interact with those services.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality, permissions, legal requirements or privacy practices change. The latest version will remain available on this page.

Contact

For privacy or support enquiries, please use the official developer support contact shown on the application's app-store listing.
