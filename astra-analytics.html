Privacy Policy — ASTRA ANALYTICS

Effective date: 23 August 2026

Last updated: 23 August 2026

ASTRA ANALYTICS is a local-first data analysis application designed to help users inspect, analyze and work with spreadsheet and related data files.

User-Selected Files

ASTRA ANALYTICS may access files only when the user selects or opens them through supported file-selection features. The application is not designed to scan unrelated files on the device without user action.

Local Processing

Core analysis is designed to take place on the user's device. Spreadsheet contents, calculations, summaries and generated analysis are processed locally where supported by the application.

Saved Data and History

The application may store settings, analysis history, saved datasets, preferences or generated outputs locally on the device so that users can continue or reopen their work.

File Export

Users may choose to export analysis results or generated files. Exported content is saved or shared only when initiated by the user.

Personal and Business Data

Files selected by users may contain personal, business, operational or other information. ASTRA ANALYTICS processes such information only as required to provide the requested analysis functionality.

Internet and Cloud Processing

The core application is designed to operate without requiring cloud upload for local analysis. If a future feature introduces online processing or third-party services that handle user data, this policy will be updated to explain that processing.

Device Permissions

The application may request file or storage-related access only when necessary for user-selected import, export or sharing functionality.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used in the design of the application. Users are responsible for protecting access to their devices and sensitive files stored on them.

Third-Party Services

Some platform-level file selection, storage or sharing functions may depend on Android or other installed applications. Their own privacy practices may apply when users choose to interact with them.

Advertising and Tracking

ASTRA ANALYTICS is not designed to require behavioural advertising or unnecessary cross-app tracking for its core analytics functionality.

Children's Privacy

ASTRA ANALYTICS is a general productivity and data-analysis application and is not specifically designed for children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, legal requirements or privacy practices change. The latest version will remain available on this page.

Contact

For privacy or support enquiries, please use the official developer support contact shown on the application's app-store listing.
