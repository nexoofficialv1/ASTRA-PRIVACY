ASTRA LOCAL Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

ASTRA LOCAL is a local service discovery and business listing application that helps users find service providers and businesses in their area.

Information Used

Depending on the features used, ASTRA LOCAL may process business or service-provider information such as name, phone number, service category, service area, business details, offers, ratings and comments.

User and Provider Information

Service-provider and business contact information may be displayed in the app where the provider has supplied or authorized publication of that information, or where it has been collected for directory purposes with appropriate consent.

Ratings and Feedback

Users may submit ratings, comments or contact/service experience feedback. Such feedback may be visible to other users and to the relevant provider when the provider manages or claims the listing.

Location

ASTRA LOCAL may use location or service-area information to improve local search and discovery. Location access is used only where required by enabled features and subject to device permission controls.

Calls, Sharing and External Apps

Users may choose to call a listed provider, open WhatsApp, share business information or use other external applications. Those third-party applications operate under their own privacy policies.

Backend and Network Services

ASTRA LOCAL may use online backend services to store, synchronize or retrieve directory information, business profiles, offers, ratings and related application data.

Data Security

Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect application data. Users and businesses remain responsible for protecting their own devices, accounts and information.

Business Listings

Businesses or service providers may request corrections, updates or removal of their listing information where appropriate.

Children

ASTRA LOCAL is a general local-services application and is not specifically designed for children.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, data practices or legal requirements change.

Contact

For privacy, listing correction or support requests, please use the developer contact information shown on the app-store listing.
